The 2026 Technical Revolution

Everything you need to know about Formula 1's biggest regulatory overhaul in modern history.

01

Active Aerodynamics

Replacing traditional DRS: Cars switch between high-downforce cornering (Z-Mode) and low-drag straightaway running (X-Mode) with moving front and rear wing flaps.

02

50% Electric 350kW MGU-K

Electric output triples from 120kW to 350kW. The complex MGU-H is abolished, making power units more accessible for new manufacturers like Audi and Cadillac.

03

100% Sustainable Fuel

Internal combustion engines now run strictly on advanced drop-in synthetic e-fuels or non-food bio-fuels with zero net fossil carbon footprint.
